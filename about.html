Our Story

Care That Feels Like Family

Safe Haven Home Care began by serving individuals with developmental disabilities and their families across Greater Cincinnati. Earning the trust of those families meant showing up every day with patience, consistency and genuine respect for each person's choices. We still work that way.

That foundation shaped who we are. Now we're opening our doors to seniors who want to age in place and to adults recovering from surgery or a hospital stay who need extra help while they heal. The people we serve have changed, but our promise hasn't: care that puts safety first and treats every client like family.

The DoDD Difference

Held to a Higher Standard

Most private home care agencies set their own rules. As an Ohio Department of Developmental Disabilities (DoDD)-certified provider, we answer to the state, and every client benefits from it.

  • State-required criminal background checks for every caregiver
  • Abuse and registry screenings before anyone enters your home
  • State-mandated training plus ongoing continuing education
  • Documented care, incident reporting and regular compliance reviews

Whether you choose us for a parent, for yourself after surgery or for a loved one on a DoDD waiver, you get the same accountability.

Our Mission

Our Mission at Safe Haven Home Care is to support independence and enrich the lives of the individuals we serve. Through compassionate, person-centered non-medical care, we empower seniors, those recovering from surgery, and individuals with developmental disabilities across Greater Cincinnati to live safely, comfortably, and with true dignity at home.
