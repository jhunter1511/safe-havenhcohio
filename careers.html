Careers

Make a Difference Every Day

We're hiring compassionate, dependable caregivers and Direct Support Professionals (DSPs), and we offer paid training for every DSP on our team. We serve Hamilton, Butler, Warren, Clermont and Clinton Counties.

Why Work With Us

  • Flexible scheduling: full-time, part-time and weekends
  • Competitive pay
  • Paid DSP training for everyone on our team
  • Ongoing support and continuing education
  • Consistent clients so you can build real relationships
  • A local team that values and respects caregivers

Who We're Looking For

  • Direct Support Professionals (DSPs) for DoDD waiver services. New to the field? We offer paid training
  • DSPs with Med 1 and Med 2 certification
  • Personal Care Aides, STNAs and Home Health Aides
  • Companion Caregivers for seniors and post-surgery clients
  • House Leads to support and guide our DSP teams
  • Office Administrators and Office Managers
  • Reliable transportation and a caring heart

Apply in 3 Easy Steps

  1. Fill out the online application below. It takes about 10 minutes, and works on your phone.
  2. We'll call you within 24–48 hours to talk about the role and set up an interview.
  3. Interview and paid training. No experience? No problem. We'll train you.
