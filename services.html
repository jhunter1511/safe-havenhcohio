Our Services

Personalized Support for a More Independent Tomorrow

Non-medical, person-centered care across Hamilton, Butler, Warren, Clermont and Clinton Counties. Book by the hour, the day or around the clock, and change your plan as your needs change.

Personal Care

Gentle, respectful help with bathing, grooming, dressing and mobility. We protect your dignity while keeping you safe and comfortable.

  • Bathing, showering and hygiene
  • Grooming, dressing and toileting
  • Mobility, transfers and fall prevention
  • Incontinence care

Companion Care

Real conversation, shared hobbies and outings around town. We ease loneliness and bring joy back into everyday life.

  • Conversation, games and hobbies
  • Walks and light activity
  • Rides and company at appointments and errands
  • Community and social outings

Respite Support

Caring for a loved one is a full-time job, and everyone needs a break. We give senior families and DoDD waiver families trusted relief for a few hours, overnight or longer.

  • Short visits, overnights or multi-day coverage
  • We keep your loved one's routine steady
  • Available to private-pay and DoDD waiver families

Daily Living Assistance

Meal prep, medication reminders, light housekeeping and errands. We keep the household running so you can focus on living well, or on healing.

  • Meal planning and preparation
  • Medication reminders
  • Light housekeeping and laundry
  • Grocery shopping and errands
  • Transitional support after surgery or a hospital discharge

DoDD Waiver Services

Certified support for individuals on Ohio's IO, Level One and SELF waivers. We work alongside families and SSAs to deliver person-centered services that honor each individual's goals.

  • Individual Options (IO) Waiver
  • Level One Waiver
  • Self-Empowered Life Funding (SELF) Waiver
  • Homemaker/Personal Care, respite and community integration support
  • Medication administration by Med 1 & Med 2 certified staff

Patient-Directed HPC

Patient-Directed Homemaker/Personal Care puts the individual and their family in charge. You help choose your caregivers, set the schedule and decide how support is delivered, and we handle the training, compliance and paperwork behind the scenes.

  • Available through IO, Level One and SELF waivers
  • Caregivers chosen with, and trusted by, the individual and family
  • Support shaped around personal goals and daily routines

Medication Administration

Our Direct Support Professionals hold Ohio DoDD Medication Administration certification, Category 1 (Med 1) and Category 2 (Med 2). That means the right medication, at the right time, given by trained staff who know the individual, with every dose documented.

  • Med 1: oral, topical and other routine medications
  • Med 2: gastrostomy and jejunostomy (G/J) tube medications and feedings
  • Accurate medication records, following state requirements
  • Available with DoDD waiver services

24/7 Care

When it's no longer safe to be alone, we're there around the clock. Rotating caregivers make sure someone fresh, alert and familiar is always in the home, day and night.

  • Continuous supervision and fall prevention
  • Personal care, meals and companionship at any hour
  • A consistent care team who knows your routine
  • Available to private-pay clients and waiver families

Non-Medical Transportation

Safe, friendly rides around Greater Cincinnati with a caregiver who stays with you, not just a driver who drops you off. We'll get you to appointments, errands and the people you love.

  • Doctor's appointments and therapy visits
  • Pharmacy, grocery and errand runs
  • Church, family visits and community outings
  • Available to private-pay clients and waiver families
